Layout: one large pose on the left (floating, waving one fin, a small water spout), three smaller poses on the right: (1) excited, leaping up with a big water spout, (2) flustered with sweat drops, (3) asleep with closed eyes and a tiny bubble from the blowhole.
No text, no labels. No orange, no light blue, no green on the whale. Nothing stacked on its back, no boxes, no containers.
